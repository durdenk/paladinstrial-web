Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Paladin's Trial mobile application (the "App"), created by Cukka Creatives (the "Service Provider"). It explains what information is processed when you use the App. The App does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, or other contact details.

1. Information collected

The Service Provider does not run its own servers for the App and does not directly collect personal information. Some information is processed by the third-party services built into the App:

  • Advertising data (Google AdMob): When ads are requested or shown, Google AdMob may process your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location inferred from the IP address, device and operating-system information, app information, consent choices, and ad-interaction data (impressions, clicks, and earned rewards).
  • Purchase data (Google Play Billing): If you buy Remove Ads, Google Play processes the transaction and gives the App the purchase state and a purchase token so the purchase can be confirmed and restored. The Service Provider never receives your payment-card details.
  • In-app review (Google Play): The App may ask Google Play to show its rating dialog. Ratings and reviews you submit are handled by Google Play under its own policies.

The App does not collect precise location, contacts, photos, files, or microphone or camera data.

2. Data stored on your device

Your game progress is stored only in the App's private storage on your device. This includes unlocked heroes, completed maps, gold and gems, permanent upgrade levels, daily reward status and dates, whether ads have been removed, and the date of the last review request. This data is not sent to the Service Provider.

3. Permissions

  • Internet and network access: Used to load ads, process purchases, and request the in-app review dialog. The core game is playable offline.
  • Billing: Used by Google Play Billing for the optional Remove Ads purchase.
  • Advertising ID: Used by Google AdMob for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

4. Advertising

The App shows optional rewarded ads (for daily rewards, extra gold at the end of a level, and a revive) and may show interstitial ads after level rewards. Where required by law, you may be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. Purchasing Remove Ads stops ads from being shown in the App.

5. Third-party services

The following services have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Payments

The Service Provider may disclose information if required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process, or when it believes in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect its rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, or investigate fraud.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Game progress: Clear the App's data in Android Settings or uninstall the App to delete all locally stored progress. This cannot be undone and is not backed up by the App.
  • Purchases: Uninstalling the App does not delete your Remove Ads purchase; it is restored from your Google Play account.
  • Third-party data: Data processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies. For other questions, email cukkacreatives@gmail.com and the Service Provider will respond within a reasonable time.

You can stop all processing by uninstalling the App.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and the Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, contact cukkacreatives@gmail.com so the necessary steps can be taken.

8. Security

The Service Provider takes reasonable care to protect the information it handles. Because game progress stays on your device and third-party data is handled by Google, the security of that data also depends on your device and those providers.

9. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

If you have questions about privacy while using the App, contact Cukka Creatives at cukkacreatives@gmail.com.
